If you've been scrolling through K-pop content and keep bumping into **ITZY** and **NMIXX** without quite knowing who's who, you're in good company — and this ITZY vs NMIXX guide to JYP's girl groups is exactly what you need. Both groups come from the same powerhouse label, [JYP Entertainment](https://www.jype.com/), yet they carved out strikingly different artistic identities. By the end of this piece, you'll know each group's vibe, every member's name, their biggest tracks, and — most importantly — how to figure out which fandom you're about to fall headfirst into.

## What Makes ITZY and NMIXX Different? (The One-Line Verdict)

The single clearest way to separate ITZY and NMIXX is this: **ITZY is about unshakeable self-confidence, while NMIXX is about sonic chaos in the best possible way.** I remember the first time I played ITZY's "DALLA DALLA" back-to-back with NMIXX's "O.O" for a friend who was new to K-pop — her face said it all. ITZY felt like a runway walk; NMIXX felt like someone changed the radio station mid-song on purpose, and it somehow worked. That contrast is the whole story in miniature.

## Meet the Members — ITZY's Five

ITZY debuted on **February 12, 2019**, and the five-member lineup has stayed rock-solid ever since, which is actually rarer than you'd think in K-pop.

1. **Yeji** — leader and main dancer; her sharp, hawk-like stage presence is instantly recognizable.
2. **Lia** — main vocalist with a warm, airy tone that cuts through the group's edgier production.
3. **Ryujin** — center and face of the group; her charisma in performance videos routinely goes viral.
4. **Chaeryeong** — main dancer who trained for years before debut; her technique is meticulous.
5. **Yuna** — maknae (youngest) and arguably the group's biggest current solo breakout, thanks to a string of brand deals and acting projects as of mid-2026.

When I first got into ITZY, I made the rookie mistake of thinking Ryujin and Yeji were the same person (the internet has never let me live this down). Give it two or three music videos and you'll clock everyone instantly.

## Meet the Members — NMIXX's Six

NMIXX debuted on **February 22, 2022**, with seven members, but Jinni departed in December 2022. The current six-member lineup is:

1. **Lily** — main vocalist; Korean-American from Los Angeles, which gives her a natural English-language presence that international fans love.
2. **Haewon** — lead vocalist with expressive, theatrical delivery.
3. **Sullyoon** — widely cited as one of the visuals of her generation; her modeling-level looks trend on social media constantly.
4. **Kyujin** — main dancer; her freestyle clips on social media have their own dedicated following.
5. **Bae** — lead dancer and vocalist; known for her powerful stage energy.
6. **Jiwoo** — maknae; vocally the group's secret weapon, with a range that surprises first-time listeners.

NMIXX is a group where I genuinely recommend watching a live performance *before* a music video, because the vocal ability hits differently when you see it unfiltered.

## The Music: ITZY's Discography at a Glance

ITZY's catalog is built around a thesis: **you don't need anyone's approval.** Their debut single "DALLA DALLA" (which translates roughly to "different different") set the template — bright but defiant production, lyrics that tell you to stop comparing yourself to others, choreography that looks like it was designed to go viral (because it was).

Key tracks to start with, in order:

1. **"DALLA DALLA" (2019)** — the debut; aggressive confidence, zero apology.
2. **"WANNABE" (2020)** — probably their most globally streamed song; the "I wanna be me" chorus is stuck in my head as I type this.
3. **"LOCO" (2021)** — slightly softer, summer-ready, a good entry point if the earlier stuff feels too intense.
4. **"CAKE" (2022)** — playful, brighter palette, shows their range.
5. **"BORN TO BE" (2024)** — their most recent full album era; muscular production, every member gets a showcase moment.

You can find their full official releases on [JYP Entertainment's YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@JYPE), which posts every MV and performance clip as of August 2026.

## The Music: NMIXX and the "Mix Pop" Concept

NMIXX's defining artistic move is something JYP called **"Mix Pop"** — songs that deliberately shift genre, tempo, or mood mid-track. The debut single "O.O" is the textbook example: it opens like a pristine pop song, then abruptly pivots into something harder, then back again. Casual listeners either find it thrilling or disorienting. Committed fans find it addictive.

Key tracks to start with:

1. **"O.O" (2022)** — chaotic introduction; watch the performance video first.
2. **"DICE" (2022)** — smoother entry point; more cohesive, great showcase for Sullyoon and Lily.
3. **"Young, Dumb, Stupid" (2023)** — this one surprised a lot of skeptics; proper bop with a more conventional structure.
4. **"DASH" (2024)** — high-energy, stadium-ready; the one I'd play for a friend who's only ever heard Western pop.
5. **"Fe3O4: STICK OUT" (2024)** — their most ambitious era yet, with a title that doubles as a chemistry reference (iron oxide — get it?).

NMIXX updates their official content regularly through [JYP Entertainment](https://www.jype.com/), so check there for the latest as of 2026.

## MIDZY vs NSWER: What It's Like Inside Each Fandom

Fandoms have personalities, and knowing them before you join helps. Here's my honest read after spending time in both communities online.

**MIDZY** (ITZY's fandom) skews slightly older — a lot of fans who came up with ITZY during the 2019–2020 era are now in their mid-to-late twenties. The community has a protective, big-sibling energy. Fan projects for member birthdays and charity streams are common. The vibe on social media is confident and a little irreverent, which fits the group's whole brand.

**NSWER** (NMIXX's fandom) is younger on average and extremely online in a different way — there's a lot of music-theory discussion, reaction threads to "Mix Pop" song structures, and passionate debate about which era is the best. If you love digging into the *why* of music, NSWER threads will keep you busy for hours.

Both fandoms have an active international presence, particularly in the US, Southeast Asia, and the Philippines. K-pop industry tracker [Hanteo Chart](https://www.hanteo.com/) regularly shows both groups performing strongly in global album sales as of mid-2026.

## How to Pick Your Group (Or Just Stan Both)

Look, nobody says you have to choose — but if you're a new fan trying to find your footing, here's a practical framework:

- **Go ITZY if:** you want anthems that make you feel invincible, you love tight formations in choreography, or you want a discography you can listen to chronologically like a coming-of-age story.
- **Go NMIXX if:** you're a music nerd who loves genre experimentation, you want to be surprised by a song's structure, or Lily's English-language charisma is your entry point.
- **Go both if:** you have unlimited time and a Spotify Premium account (no judgment — I did exactly this).

A practical starter playlist I'd actually recommend: alternate between ITZY's "WANNABE" and NMIXX's "DICE" for a week. By day seven, you'll know exactly where you land.
