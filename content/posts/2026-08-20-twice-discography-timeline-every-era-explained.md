If you've recently fallen down the TWICE rabbit hole and feel overwhelmed by their **twice discography** — don't worry, I've been there. When I first started exploring their **twice albums** and trying to understand each of the **twice eras**, it felt like walking into the middle of a ten-season TV show. But once you map it out chronologically, the story clicks beautifully. This guide will walk you through every major era, from their 2015 debut to their 2026 releases, so you can listen with full context and actually *feel* the growth.

> **Quick note:** Streaming links, chart positions, and tour dates change frequently. Always check [TWICE's official website](https://twice.jype.com) and [JYP Entertainment's official channels](https://www.jype.com) for the most current information.

## Era 1: The "Candy Pop" Debut Years (2015–2017) — Where It All Began

TWICE burst onto the scene in October 2015 as JYP Entertainment's nine-member multinational girl group, and their debut era is all about infectious, unapologetic fun. Their first extended play, ***The Story Begins***, dropped with lead single **"Like OOH-AHH"** — a track that racked up over 100 million YouTube views faster than almost any K-pop debut at the time (as of August 2026, it sits comfortably past 300 million views). What made this era special wasn't just the catchy hooks; it was the group's genuine charisma. Nine girls — Nayeon, Jeongyeon, Momo, Sana, Jihyo, Mina, Dahyun, Chaeyoung, and Tzuyu — each with a distinct personality that fans could immediately latch onto.

The debut era expanded quickly:

| Release | Title | Lead Single |
|---|---|---|
| *The Story Begins* (Oct 2015) | EP | "Like OOH-AHH" |
| *Page Two* (Apr 2016) | EP | "Cheer Up" |
| *TWICEcoaster: LANE 1* (Oct 2016) | EP | "TT" |
| *Signal* (May 2017) | EP | "Signal" |
| *Twicetagram* (Oct 2017) | EP | "Likey" |

**"Cheer Up" and "TT" won Song of the Year at major Korean awards ceremonies** and cemented TWICE as the dominant girl group of the mid-2010s. If you start anywhere, start with **"TT"** — the Halloween-themed MV is iconic, the choreography is instantly recognizable, and it perfectly captures this era's playful energy.

---

## Era 2: The Peak Domination Era (2018–2019) — Polished, Powerful, Everywhere

By 2018, TWICE weren't just popular — they were *inescapable* in Korea. This era marked their artistic polish and commercial peak on home turf. Their first full-length studio album, ***TWICEtagram*** had already shown depth, but ***What is Love?*** (April 2018) and ***YES or YES*** (November 2018) took things further with slicker production and more confident vocal performances.

The standout of this entire era, for me, is **"Dance the Night Away"** — a summer bop with a stranded-island MV that felt genuinely cinematic. Then came ***FANCY YOU*** (April 2019), which I'd call the pivot point: **"FANCY"** introduced a slightly retro, more sophisticated sound that hinted TWICE were ready to evolve. 

Key releases:

| Release | Title | Notable Track |
|---|---|---|
| *What is Love?* (Apr 2018) | EP | "What is Love?" |
| *Summer Nights* (Aug 2018) | EP | "Dance the Night Away" |
| *YES or YES* (Nov 2018) | EP | "YES or YES" |
| *FANCY YOU* (Apr 2019) | EP | "FANCY" |
| *Feel Special* (Sep 2019) | EP | "Feel Special" |

**"Feel Special"** deserves a special mention — written during a period when several members faced health challenges, it became one of the most emotionally resonant songs in their catalog and showed a more vulnerable, genuine side of the group.

---

## Era 3: The Global Pivot & English Singles (2020–2021) — TWICE Goes International

When the pandemic reshaped the music industry, TWICE used the downtime strategically. **2020–2021 is the era where TWICE became a global act**, not just a Korean phenomenon. They released their first English-language single, **"The Feels"** (October 2021), which charted on the *Billboard* Hot 100 — a milestone for any K-pop group.

Their second full-length studio album, ***Eyes Wide Open*** (October 2020), was a creative statement: the production leaned into R&B, funk, and dance-pop influences far removed from their bubbly debut sound. Tracks like **"I CAN'T STOP ME"** and **"Hell in Heaven"** showed real artistic ambition.

| Release | Title | Highlight |
|---|---|---|
| *More & More* (Jun 2020) | EP | "More & More" (mystic concept) |
| *Eyes Wide Open* (Oct 2020) | Full Album | "I CAN'T STOP ME" |
| *Taste of Love* (Jun 2021) | EP | "Alcohol-Free" |
| *Formula of Love* (Nov 2021) | Full Album | "The Feels" (English single) |

I remember when **"The Feels"** dropped — international fans who'd never engaged with K-pop were suddenly sharing it. It was TWICE's clearest signal that they were building a career beyond the Korean market, and it worked.

---

## Era 4: The Mature, Self-Assured Sound (2022–2023) — READY TO BE

This is the era that made long-time fans emotional and converted the skeptics. ***BETWEEN 1&2*** (August 2022) and ***READY TO BE*** (March 2023) are widely considered **TWICE's most artistically cohesive releases**. The production is sleeker, the vocal arrangements more layered, and the group sounds like nine women fully in control of their artistry.

**"SET ME FREE"**, the lead single from *READY TO BE*, was a genuine departure — a driving, rock-adjacent banger with choreography that's arguably their most technically demanding ever. It debuted at **#4 on the Billboard Global 200** (as of March 2023), their highest charting achievement at the time.

The group also completed their first stadium tour in the US during this era — **TWICE 5TH WORLD TOUR 'READY TO BE'** — selling out venues like SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles. You can find official tour archives on [TWICE's official site](https://twice.jype.com).

---

## Era 5: Evolution & New Chapters (2024–2026) — Who TWICE Are Now

Coming into 2024 and beyond, TWICE entered a new phase shaped by individual member growth, solo projects, and contract renewals — most members re-signed with JYP Entertainment in late 2023, confirming the group's longevity. Their releases in 2024 and into 2025–2026 have experimented with bolder sonic territories: atmospheric mid-tempos, anthemic pop, and deeper emotional narratives.

As of August 2026, TWICE continue to release music and perform globally, with Jihyo, Nayeon, Chaeyoung, and others having released acclaimed solo projects that feed back into fans' appreciation of the group dynamic. For the latest releases and tour announcements, **always check [JYP Entertainment's official page](https://www.jype.com) and TWICE's verified social channels**, as schedules update frequently.

This era is still being written — which, honestly, is the most exciting part.

## How to Start Listening: A New Fan's Playlist Roadmap

If you've read this far and want a practical entry point, here's a numbered listening order I'd genuinely recommend:

1. **"TT"** — understand why they dominated 2016–2017
2. **"FANCY"** — hear the sound start to evolve
3. **"Feel Special"** — discover their emotional depth
4. **"I CAN'T STOP ME"** — meet their experimental side
5. **"The Feels"** — the crossover moment
6. **"SET ME FREE"** — hear who they are now
7. **Dive into full albums** — *Eyes Wide Open* and *READY TO BE* are the best start-to-finish listens

From there, explore member solo releases and deep-cut B-sides — that's where the real fandom begins.

## Key Takeaways: TWICE Discography at a Glance

- **Debut era (2015–2017):** Candy pop, high energy, instant hook singles — start with "TT" and "Cheer Up"
- **Peak era (2018–2019):** Commercial dominance, polished production — "FANCY" is the must-listen pivot
- **Global era (2020–2021):** R&B/dance-pop experimentation, English singles, Billboard charting
- **Mature era (2022–2023):** Artistic peak, stadium tours, "SET ME FREE" as a career-defining moment
- **Current era (2024–2026):** Ongoing evolution, solo projects, and confirmed group longevity
