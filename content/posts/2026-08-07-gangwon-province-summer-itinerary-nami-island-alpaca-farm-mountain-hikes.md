Gangwon Province in summer is, without question, one of the best hidden gems near Seoul — and if you haven't done this trip yet, you're leaving serious K-travel points on the table. Within two to three hours of the capital you get lush river islands, mountain air so clean it feels almost illegal, and animals that will absolutely wreck your camera roll. I've done this loop more times than I can count, and this guide is my honest, street-level breakdown of how to pull it off in three or four days without the tourist-trap headaches.

---

## Why Gangwon Province Should Be Your Summer Korea Destination Right Now

Gangwon is the region that rewards spontaneity but punishes under-preparation — this section is your orientation map. Most international visitors treat Gangwon as the ski-season province (PyeongChang 2018 Olympics, anyone?), but summer here is a completely different animal. Average temperatures in the valleys sit around **24–28 °C** during July and August (as of August 2026), while the mountain ridges of Seoraksan and Odaesan stay a bliss-inducing 5–8 °C cooler than Seoul. The rivers are full, the forests are an almost aggressive shade of green, and the crowds — while real — are a fraction of what Jeju or Busan sees.

The province spans the entire northeastern chunk of the Korean peninsula, so it helps to mentally split it into **West Gangwon** (Chuncheon, Nami Island, Spring Valley) and **East Gangwon** (Sokcho, Seoraksan, Gangneung coast). This itinerary focuses on West Gangwon first, then dips into the east — a route you can drive or piece together by public transport.

For big-picture context on the region, [VisitKorea's Gangwon section](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/enu/ATR/SI_EN_3_1_1_1.jsp) is a solid first stop, and [Korea.net's travel features](https://www.korea.net/TravelTourism/Travellog/View?articleId=355) regularly highlight seasonal highlights worth bookmarking.

## Nami Island: More Than Just a K-Drama Backdrop

Nami Island — officially **Naminara Republic** — earns its place on every Gangwon itinerary because it genuinely delivers in every season, but summer turns the famous metasequoia and poplar allées into full green tunnels. When I first walked the "Chuncheon Nami Island" lane in early August, I understood immediately why *Winter Sonata* shot here — the light through those trees is cinematic even on a cloudy day.

**Getting there:** Take the ITX-Chuncheon train from Seoul Yongsan or Cheongnyangni stations (about 70–80 minutes, **₩5,800–₩8,800** as of August 2026) to Gapyeong station, then a short taxi or local bus to the ferry dock. The ferry itself is a 5-minute crossing and costs **₩18,000 round-trip** for adults (entrance included — as of August 2026; always verify at the [official Nami Island site](https://www.namisum.com/en/)).

| Item | Cost (as of Aug 2026) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ITX-Chuncheon (Seoul → Gapyeong) | ₩5,800–₩8,800 | Book via Korail app |
| Ferry + entrance (adult) | ₩18,000 | Round-trip included |
| Ferry + entrance (child 3–12) | ₩10,000 | Under 3 free |
| Bicycle rental on island | ₩3,000–₩8,000/hr | Multiple spots on island |

**Practical tips:**
1. Arrive before 10 a.m. or after 4 p.m. to dodge peak crowds in summer.
2. Bring a light rain jacket — afternoon showers are common in July/August.
3. The island bans single-use plastic, so carry a reusable bottle.
4. Give yourself at least 2–3 hours; the south end of the island near the reed fields is quieter and genuinely beautiful.

## Alpaca World: The Hidden Gem Near Chuncheon You'll Tell Everyone About

Alpaca World is hands-down the most underrated stop in West Gangwon — most travelers fly past it, which is exactly why you shouldn't. Located in Hongcheon (about 30 minutes from Chuncheon by car, or reachable by intercity bus), [Alpaca World](https://alpacaworld.co.kr/) is a working alpaca farm-turned-petting zoo set against rolling hills. And yes, it's every bit as chaotic and joyful as it sounds.

When I visited on a weekday morning in summer, I ended up hand-feeding a herd of about 20 alpacas, watching sheep graze across a valley that could have been lifted straight out of New Zealand, and catching a feeding show that had the whole crowd — mostly Korean families and young couples — absolutely losing it over a particularly aggressive alpaca named (according to his name tag) "Kevin."

**What to know before you go:**

- **Entrance fee:** Approximately **₩15,000–₩18,000** for adults (as of August 2026; check the official site for current pricing and seasonal promotions).
- **Animal feeding packages** are sold separately (around ₩3,000–₩5,000 extra) and are 100% worth it.
- Opening hours vary by season — summer hours are generally **9 a.m.–6 p.m.**, but confirm before visiting.
- The farm is hilly, so wear comfortable shoes — not sandals.
- **Getting there without a car:** Take a bus from Chuncheon Terminal toward Hongcheon; the farm offers a shuttle from the nearest stop on weekends. Alternatively, join a day-tour from Seoul that bundles Nami Island + Alpaca World — these are widely available on platforms like Klook.

## Mountain Hikes in Gangwon: From Easy Walks to Real Summits

Gangwon's mountains are the spine of the whole province, and even a modest hike here resets something in your nervous system that city life has clearly broken. The range of difficulty is enormous — from a 40-minute forest walk to a full-day ridge traverse — so there's genuinely something for every fitness level.

### My top three summer hike picks:

**1. Seoraksan National Park (Sokcho area) — Ulsanbawi Rock Loop**
This is the showpiece trail: **6 km round-trip**, roughly **4–5 hours**, with a steel staircase climb at the end that delivers jaw-dropping granite rock formations. The trailhead starts near Sinheungsa Temple. Entrance fee is **₩3,500** for adults (as of August 2026). Note that peak summer weekends see long queues at the staircase — start before 8 a.m. Check [Korea National Park Service](https://english.knps.or.kr/) for trail conditions and reservation requirements (Seoraksan sometimes requires online pre-booking during peak season).

**2. Odaesan National Park — Woljeongsa Temple Forest Walk**
For something meditative rather than strenuous, the 9 km fir-tree trail between Woljeongsa and Sangwonsa temples is arguably the most peaceful walk in all of Korea. **Flat, shaded, and free** (temple entrance ₩4,000). Perfect on a hot afternoon.

**3. Chiaksan National Park — Birobong Peak**
The underdog of Gangwon hiking, Chiaksan near Wonju rewards with far fewer crowds and serious ridge scenery. The main Birobong trail is **12 km round-trip** and takes **6–7 hours** — a proper mountain day. Entrance fee is **₩1,600** (as of August 2026).

| Trail | Distance | Difficulty | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ulsanbawi, Seoraksan | 6 km RT | Moderate–Hard | Dramatic scenery, rock formations |
| Woljeongsa Forest Walk, Odaesan | 9 km one-way | Easy | Peaceful, shaded, families |
| Birobong, Chiaksan | 12 km RT | Hard | Fewer crowds, serious hikers |

## Getting Around Gangwon Province: Transport Tips for 2026

The honest truth about Gangwon transport is that a rental car unlocks the province fully — but public transport is surprisingly workable if you plan ahead. Here's how I approach it depending on the group:

**By train + bus (no car):**
- Seoul → Chuncheon: ITX-Chuncheon from Yongsan (fastest, book on [Korail](https://www.letskorail.com/ebizbf/EbizBfMain.do)).
- Chuncheon → Sokcho: Intercity express bus from Chuncheon Bus Terminal (~2 hrs, ₩12,000 as of August 2026).
- Seoul → Sokcho direct: Express bus from Seoul Dong Seoul Terminal or Sinbundang Line's Gangnam bus hub (~2.5–3 hrs).

**By rental car:**
Renting from Seoul (pick up near Cheongnyangni or at Incheon Airport) gives you total flexibility. The Gyeongchun Expressway and Route 44 through the mountains are genuinely beautiful drives. Budget **₩60,000–₩100,000/day** for a compact rental plus fuel (as of August 2026).

**Day tours from Seoul:**
If you only have one day, several operators bundle Nami Island + Petite France or Nami Island + Alpaca World. Prices typically run **₩50,000–₩80,000** per person including transport. Check Klook or Viator for current options — always verify inclusions before booking.

## Practical Pre-Trip Checklist for Your Gangwon Summer Trip

Before you pack your hiking boots and head for the train station, run through this:

- [ ] **Book ITX-Chuncheon tickets** in advance on peak summer weekends — they sell out.
- [ ] **Check Seoraksan reservation requirements** at [Korea National Park Service](https://english.knps.or.kr/) — the park has introduced crowd management measures.
- [ ] **Confirm Alpaca World hours and shuttle availability** directly on their [official site](https://alpacaworld.co.kr/) — seasonal changes happen.
- [ ] **Pack layers** — mountain temps drop fast after sunset even in August.
- [ ] **Download Naver Maps** (not just Google Maps) — it handles Korean bus routes far better.
- [ ] **Carry cash** — smaller trailhead shops and farm stalls often don't accept foreign cards.
- [ ] **Check weather forecasts** the night before any mountain hike — summer storms can close trails.

> **One-line takeaway to save and share:** Gangwon Province gives you Nami Island's romance, alpaca chaos, and mountain silence all within a 3-hour drive of Seoul — and summer 2026 is the season to finally do it.
